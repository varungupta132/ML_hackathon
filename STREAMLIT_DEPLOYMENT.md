## **📋 Step-by-Step Deployment Process:**

### **Step 1: Go to Streamlit Cloud**
Visit: **https://share.streamlit.io/**

### **Step 2: Sign In**
- Click **"Sign in"** (top right)
- Choose **"Continue with GitHub"**
- Authorize Streamlit to access your repos

### **Step 3: Deploy New App**
1. Click **"New app"** button
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Repository**: `varungupta132/ML_hackathon`
   - **Branch**: `main`
   - **Main file path**: `app.py`
3. Click **"Deploy!"**

### **Step 4: Wait for Deployment (2-3 minutes)**
- Streamlit will install all dependencies
- Set up Tesseract OCR automatically
- Build and launch your app

### **Step 5: Get Your Live URL! 🎉**
Your app will be live at something like:
```
https://varungupta132-ml-hackathon-app-xxxxx.streamlit.app
```
